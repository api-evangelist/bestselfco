## Buying Guide (by Intent)

| Shopper intent | First recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 90-day goal sprint | Self Journal | Structured daily/weekly system for a single big goal |
| Familiar weekly planner | BestSelf Planner | Weekly cadence, lower friction than a 90-day commitment |
| Just today, nothing big | Daily Action Pad or Weekly Action Pad | Low commitment, desk-friendly |
| Date night / anniversary gift | Intimacy Deck | Prompts remove the awkward "what do we talk about?" |
| Couples communication ritual | Relationship Deck | Recurring check-in prompts |
| Group icebreakers or team workshop | Icebreaker Deck | Work-safe, easy for any group |
| Values clarity for coaching | Core Values Deck | Tactile sorting exercise, great for facilitators |
| Breakup / life transition | Breakup Deck | Reflection prompts; not therapy or crisis support |
| Kids screen-free habit | Little Hero's Journal | Guided prompts, screen-free, builds confidence |
| Digital planner right now | Weekly Action Pad Digital Download or Master Your Minutes | Immediate access; confirm download terms on PDP |

### Objection Handling
- "I quit planners" → Daily Action Pad or Weekly Action Pad; save Self Journal for shoppers who want a system
- "Will conversation cards feel awkward?" → The deck supplies the prompt, removing the pressure
- "Is it work-safe?" → Icebreaker Deck for light groups; Core Values Deck for facilitated workshops
- "I need it today" → Digital downloads only; confirm delivery terms on live PDP before claiming instant
